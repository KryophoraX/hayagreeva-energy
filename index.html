The thermal problem

Heat is the limit on compute scale

  1. 01

    Problem

    AI accelerators push package heat loads beyond what air cooling can sustain without throttling.

  2. 02

    Limit

    At extreme wattage, thermal resistance at the package becomes the bottleneck between silicon and facility rejection.

  3. 03

    Solution

    Hayagreeva HEx mounts direct-to-chip — a copper-matrix cold plate that moves heat into coolant at the package boundary.

  4. 04

    Advantage

    Rated for 5,000+ W continuous removal at 0.005 °C/W — capacity stated at the package, not averaged across the rack.

Product

Meet Hayagreeva HEx

A direct-to-chip liquid-cooling cold plate engineered for next-generation NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel accelerator packages. Copper-matrix architecture with more than 4,000 m²/m³ of wetted surface expands the heat-transfer interface where it matters most.

Designed for pilot evaluation with qualified data-center operators, OEM platforms, and system integrators ahead of launch on May 28, 2026.
