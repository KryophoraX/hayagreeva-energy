Applications

Where extreme package heat shows up — and where Hayagreeva HEx is designed to remove it at the semiconductor package before facility rejection.

Application domains

HEx targets package-level continuous heat removal above 5,000 W at 0.005 °C/W rated thermal resistance. Compatibility is package-specific for NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel accelerator architectures. Images below show facility and lab environment context — not Hayagreeva product photography.

Data centers

High-density AI racks where air cooling cannot sustain continuous accelerator load. HEx targets package-level removal so facility loops can reject heat at scale.

OEM platforms

Server and accelerator platforms that need package-specific cold-plate geometry for NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel architectures — mounting, connectors, and fluid interfaces matched to the target package.

HPC

High-performance computing environments where sustained thermal headroom protects throughput under continuous scientific and AI workloads.
